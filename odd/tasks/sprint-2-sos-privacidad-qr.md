## Specs
- **S1 (US-05 — SOS con ubicación):** Citizen identifies vulnerable person (QR or face) with geolocation permission and triggers SOS; backend registers alert, notifies **only** the primary guardian (`legalRepresentative == true`) via Resend in <10s with map link and valid frontend confirmation URL, and returns confirmation + server expiry (`120s`) to citizen.
- **S2 (US-06 — Confirmación del apoderado):** Guardian opens notification/alert view (`Notificaciones` / `/notificaciones`), sees location, time, status, and clicks `"Confirmar recepción"`. Backend atomically marks alert `CONFIRMED`, cancels pending escalation without duplicates, and citizen view reflects `"La familia recibió la notificación y está en camino"`.
- **S3 (US-07 — Escalamiento automático 120s):** Backend scheduler runs independently of citizen browser. After 120s without confirmation, atomically transitions alert to `ESCALATED`, notifies auxiliary guardians (`legalRepresentative == false`), prevents duplicate notifications, and logs each attempt/error in `NotificationAudit`.
- **S4 (US-08 — Consentimiento informado y minimización estricta):**
  - Guardian must accept informed consent (checkbox unchecked by default, full privacy modal `"Consentimiento informado de privacidad"`, version `"1.0"`, timestamp) per dependent (`Badge`) before registration completes; can revoke consent (disables QR & facial search immediately) and grant new consent (re-enables facial search, QR stays deactivated until manually regenerated).
  - Public citizen views & APIs enforce strict data minimization: `"solo nombre de pila(primer nombre) pero nada mas"` — expose ONLY first name and generic help labels/actions (`"Apoderado Titular"`, `"Apoderado Auxiliar 1"`). Enable `tel:` inside `"Llamar"` buttons while keeping phone numbers hidden visually everywhere: `"habilitar el enlace tel: dentro de los botones \"Llamar\" (ocultando el número visualmente), tambien no hay que mostrar los numeros de telefono en ningun momento"`. Auxiliary `tel:` links + `tel:114` are revealed only once `ESCALATED`.
- **S5 (US-09 — Revocación y regeneración del QR):** Guardian accesses `"Mis familiares"` from profile menu; can deactivate active QR (cannot be reactivated, invalidates old QR immediately with invalid/deactivated message) and regenerate a new QR linked to the same dependent (`Badge`), viewing/downloading the updated credential.
